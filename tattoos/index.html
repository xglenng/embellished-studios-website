Before your visit

Your questions, answered.

How do I get a tattoo estimate?

Use the tattoo request form to share your idea, approximate size, placement, and reference images. The artist reviews those details before providing a price and appointment duration.

Can I see recent tattoo work?

Our portfolio page links to current studio work on Instagram. Review examples before sending your request and include references for the style you want.

Does submitting a request book my tattoo?

No. The studio reviews your request first. Availability, the estimate, and any required deposit are arranged before your appointment is confirmed.

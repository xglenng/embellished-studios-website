How booking works

Simple from request to appointment.

Submitting a request starts the conversation. Your appointment is confirmed only after the artist or studio approves the request and any required deposit is completed.

STEP 01

Send your request

Tell us what you want, where you want it, and the timing you have in mind.

STEP 02

Review the details

We’ll ask follow-up questions, discuss design or jewelry, and confirm suitability.

STEP 03

Confirm your visit

Once availability and deposit requirements are settled, you’ll receive confirmation and preparation details.

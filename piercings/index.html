Before your visit

Your questions, answered.

How much does a piercing cost?

Pricing depends on the requested piercing and jewelry. Send a piercing request with the placement you want so the studio can follow up with pricing and appointment details.

Do I need an appointment?

Embellished Studios operates by appointment. Submit a piercing request and the studio will follow up with availability. Sending the form alone does not confirm a booking.

Can I ask about several ear piercings?

Yes. Describe the placements or overall arrangement you are considering in your request. The studio can discuss your ideas and review suitability with you.

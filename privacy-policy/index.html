Information we collect

How we use information

We may use collected information to respond to inquiries; communicate about tattoo, piercing, or training services; schedule, confirm, modify, or cancel appointments; send confirmations and reminders; provide preparation or aftercare information; operate and improve our website and services; maintain business records; prevent misuse or security incidents; and comply with legal obligations.

Customer-care SMS program

Customers may voluntarily consent to receive conversational and appointment-related text messages from Embellished Studios. Messages may include responses to inquiries, tattoo or piercing request communications, appointment scheduling, confirmations, reminders, changes, cancellations, requests for information, preparation instructions, aftercare information, and other communications directly related to requested services.

Message frequency varies based on your inquiries, bookings, appointments, and interactions with Embellished Studios. Message and data rates may apply.

Consent to receive text messages is optional and is not a condition of purchasing goods or services. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. After STOP, you may receive one confirmation message and will receive no further messages through that program unless you opt in again. For assistance, reply HELP or contact the studio at 435-668-5988 or embellished.studios.stg@gmail.com.

Mobile information and SMS consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors that provide support services may be permitted only when reasonably necessary for those providers to perform services on our behalf, and those providers may not use the information for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

All information-sharing categories described in this Policy exclude text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. Embellished Studios does not sell, rent, purchase, or disclose mobile phone numbers, text-messaging opt-in information, or text-messaging consent information for marketing or promotional purposes.

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Embellished Studios LLC, doing business as Embellished Studios (“Embellished Studios,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, submit a service request, schedule an appointment, enroll in training, or communicate with us.

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, appointment and booking information, tattoo or piercing request details, training inquiries, information submitted through forms, communications you send to us, and basic website or device information.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, review service requests, schedule and manage appointments, provide requested services, communicate about training, process payments where applicable, maintain records, improve our services and website, protect our business, and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

We may use third-party providers for website hosting, forms, scheduling, communications, payments, data storage, security, and related business operations. These providers may process information as reasonably necessary to perform services for us and are subject to their own privacy practices and contractual obligations.

Information sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers working on our behalf, when you direct or authorize us to do so, to comply with law or legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of our services.

Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including business, legal, tax, safety, and recordkeeping needs. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact us to request correction of information you provided, subject to legal and recordkeeping requirements. You may also choose which available contact method you use when reaching the studio.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to Embellished Studios LLC, 127 N 500 E, Suite D & E, Saint George, Utah 84770; 435-668-5988; or embellished.studios.stg@gmail.com.

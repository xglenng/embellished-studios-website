Piercing apprenticeship

4-Month Piercing Apprenticeship

A comprehensive 4–6 month hands-on program, held 2–3 days per week (Wednesdays & Fridays, 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM).

In the apprenticeship, you'll learn everything you need to work as a licensed body piercer, including:

  • Decontamination, sanitation & sterilization
  • Autoclave sterilization
  • Piercing techniques
  • Equipment selection
  • Client positioning & education
  • Client paperwork & record keeping
  • Business licensing
  • Marketing
  • And more!

You'll begin by shadowing me and observing live piercing models, then practice on training models. Each month, we'll progress to more advanced piercings — and by the end, you'll be performing your own piercings on models independently!

Upon completion, you will be fully prepared to work as a licensed body piercer.

The next step would be an in-person interview. Feel free to reach out with any questions.

Program investmentStarting at $4,800

Comprehensive hands-on mentorship over approximately 4–6 months.

Tattoo apprenticeship

Tattoo Apprenticeship

A comprehensive 4–6 month hands-on program with the next start date in August/September 2026.

Schedule: 2–3 days per week — Wednesdays & Fridays from 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM, plus one Saturday or Sunday per month.

Here's how the program breaks down:

  • Month 1 — Shadowing & Digital Design: You'll observe and develop your design skills
  • Month 2 — Flash Tattoos: You'll begin tattooing flash pieces with me by your side
  • Months 3 & 4 — Custom & Flash Work: You'll take on both custom pieces and flash tattoos independently alongside me

Throughout the program, you'll learn:

  • Digital design
  • Decontamination, sanitation & sterilization
  • Tattooing techniques
  • Equipment selection
  • Client positioning & education
  • Client paperwork & record keeping
  • Business licensing
  • Marketing
  • And more!

The $4,800 program fee covers all supplies used during training as well as the time I dedicate to teaching rather than seeing my own clients.

Upon completion, you'll graduate ready to take on clients and pursue your health department license. The program also covers the business and marketing fundamentals needed to build your clientele.

The next step would be an in-person interview. Feel free to reach out with any questions — I'd love to help you get started!

Weekend piercing course

Three focused days. A foundation you can use.

The weekend program combines lecture, procedure review, mannequin practice, hands-on skill development, and supervised model work. It is intended for students outside Washington and Iron County; local students should ask about apprenticeship requirements.

  • Day 1 · FundamentalsRules, regulations, sanitation, consent, jewelry, equipment, and mannequin practice.
  • Day 2 · Skill buildingTechnique review and supervised practice beginning with foundational piercings.
  • Day 3 · Model workHands-on model appointments, advanced placement introduction, debrief, and certificate completion.
Course investment$2,500

$500 nonrefundable deposit holds your place. The remaining $2,000 is due on the first day of class.

Students must be 18 or older and complete required Bloodborne Pathogens and CPR certification before the course begins. Licensing requirements vary by state and county; contact your local health department before enrolling.

Program overview

Embellished Studios customer care SMS

SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing goods or services or submitting a tattoo or piercing request. On our request forms, customers may leave the SMS checkbox unchecked and submit the same request by email instead.

  • Separate, affirmative consent
  • Request forms remain usable when SMS is declined
  • Consent control is unchecked by default
  • Message purpose disclosed before consent
  • Frequency and carrier charges disclosed
  • STOP and HELP instructions provided
  • Privacy Policy and Terms linked publicly
Keyword opt-in

Text BOOK to
435-668-5988

By texting BOOK, you agree to receive customer-care text messages from Embellished Studios about tattoo or piercing inquiries, booking requests, appointment confirmations, reminders, scheduling changes, preparation, aftercare, and related requested services. Message frequency varies based on your inquiries and appointments. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.
